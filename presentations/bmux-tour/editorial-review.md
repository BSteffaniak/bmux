# Editorial review — 2026-10-15

## Intent and fixed requirements

Tell a concrete BMUX story for technically curious terminal users/developers: organize real work, customize the workspace, understand delivery boundaries, then inspect executable playbooks and presentation composition. Audience and duration are provisional, not user-specified. Chapter depth is adjustable.

All seven playbooks are retained. JEV Flappy Bird is the intentional final slide, not an optional candidate for removal. Local BMUX does not require JEV or another remote service. Unfinished distributed features are not showcased. Architecture is selected for implemented behavior and coherent design, not attractive contracts alone.

## Review method

Three parallel lanes covered narrative/source examples, DSL improvements, and implementation readiness. The coordinator integrated their work and used the official TypeSafe `/v1/systemone` API with `jev-1.13.0` for structured choice reviews. Credentials and raw request/response evidence remain outside the repository.

JEV received the talk goal, audience assumptions, full outline, current slide and adjacent slides. Two views were deliberately distinguished:

- Full authored context, including explicitly private notes and associated demo source.
- Audience-visible content only, with notes removed. This avoids letting notes conceal missing explanations.

Questions separately assessed clarity, usefulness, interest, contextual fit, medium and editorial treatment. A three-example sanity check distinguished a concrete workflow, deliberately confusing jargon, and the required playful finale. This is **not statistical calibration**. Choice probabilities/confidence are model outputs, not validated correctness probabilities.

The coordinator made 139 editorial API requests: 3 sanity checks, 24 full-context baseline, 24 visible baseline, 26 visible initial revision, 26 full-context revision, 10 order-reversed pair comparisons, and 26 final visible reviews. The live finale rehearsal used additional game decision requests. Exact requests/results are private temporary artifacts; no total dollar cost is inferred.

Most full-context baseline decisions were KEEP even when the same answers called material abstract. Audience-only review was more critical. Therefore decisions were not automated from overall labels, no aggregate quality score is reported, and no claim of universal improvement is made. Final visible review still identifies abstract/deeper material; these are deliberate optional explanation points, not proof that every slide is equally accessible.

## Implementation confidence and limits

Selective implementation inspection found real catalog/activation wiring, revisioned preview state, output-delivery commit/repair logic, bounded public projection and explicit managed-execution admission. This is not an exhaustive defect audit or a production-readiness certification. No runtime defect was conclusively established in that audit.

Demonstration refinements use real shell/attach paths:

- Panes show fixture/manifests, kernel and clock in a checked layout.
- Navigation retains original shell identity and private fixture state across tab/workspace changes.
- Finder checks selected output/layout after real picker interaction.
- Rendering checks actual catalog output and return from scrollback; no fault-injection claim.
- Evidence checks fixture count and independent second-pane count/sum before retaining a snapshot.
- Themes and Flappy Bird remain intact.
